Our Story

About Eagles Touch

Eagles Touch began as a single stall of trusted imports and grew into one of Lagos' most relied-upon names in fragrance, built on one simple rule: never sell what we wouldn't wear ourselves. Every bottle in our atelier is sourced directly from authorized distributors, with verifiable batch codes, so what reaches your hands — or your shop shelf — is exactly what it claims to be.

Today we serve two kinds of customers under one roof: private collectors looking for a signature scent, and shop owners and resellers across Nigeria who build their businesses on our wholesale cartons. Both get the same promise — authenticity, fair pricing, and a team that answers on WhatsApp in minutes.
